# 👔 User Interview Questions – Men’s Clothing & Fashion App  

---

## 📝 1. Background & Context  
- Tell me about your usual approach to shopping for clothes.  
- How often do you shop for new clothing, and what usually triggers it?  
- Where do you typically shop (online, in-store, apps, social media)? Why?  

---

## 🎯 2. Goals & Motivations  
- What do you usually look for when buying men’s clothing (e.g., comfort, price, brand, trend, quality)?  
- What motivates you most when deciding on a purchase?  
- Can you describe what a “successful” shopping experience looks like for you?  

---

## 😖 3. Pain Points & Frustrations  
- What frustrates you the most when shopping for men’s clothing online?  
- Can you share a recent bad experience you’ve had with a fashion app or website?  
- Do you ever feel overwhelmed by choices? If yes, when does that happen?  
- How do you feel about sizing and fit when shopping online?  

---

## 📱 4. Current Behaviors & Tools  
- Which apps or websites do you currently use for men’s fashion, and what do you like/dislike about them?  
- How do you usually discover new styles or brands?  
- Walk me through your last online purchase — how did you decide what to buy?  

---
