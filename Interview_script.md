# 🎤 User Interview Checklist

---

## 📝 1. Preparation
- [ ] Define research goals (pain points, behaviors, motivations).
- [ ] Recruit participants matching target demographic.
- [ ] Choose format → 🎥 Online / 👥 In-person / 🧑‍💻 Moderated / ⚡ Unmoderated.
- [ ] Prepare interview script:
  - [ ] Intro + purpose
  - [ ] Core open-ended questions
  - [ ] Follow-up prompts (*“Can you tell me more?”*)
- [ ] Test tools (Zoom, Maze, UserTesting, recording).
- [ ] Prepare consent forms & agreements.
- [ ] Arrange incentives (gift card, voucher, etc.).

---

## 🎙️ 2. During the Interview
- [ ] Start with a warm intro → reassure it’s **not a test** of them.
- [ ] Outline session structure (30–60 min).
- [ ] Ask **open-ended questions**, e.g.:
  - “Tell me about your commute.”
  - “What frustrates you most about using transit apps?”
  - “How do you usually solve that?”
- [ ] Use **“why” questions** to dig deeper.
- [ ] Encourage storytelling (capture anecdotes).
- [ ] Avoid leading/influencing answers.
- [ ] Take notes (or record with permission).
- [ ] Thank participants sincerely.

---

## 👀 2a. Observing Behavior & Non-Verbal Cues
- [ ] Pay attention to **tone changes** or signs of tension (especially around sensitive topics like money).  
- [ ] Watch for **defensive body language**: crossed arms, lack of eye contact, hesitation.  
- [ ] Note if participants seem uncomfortable → gently probe without making them self-conscious.  
- [ ] Treat discomfort as an **opportunity to learn**, but balance with empathy.  

---

## 📊 3. After the Interview
- [ ] Transcribe notes or recordings.
- [ ] Highlight **key quotes** to illustrate insights.
- [ ] Synthesize findings:
  - Common themes
  - Frustrations
  - Goals
  - Behaviors
- [ ] Check hypotheses → validated ❌ disproved ✅.
- [ ] Share insights with stakeholders (summary doc, slides, Notion page).

---

## 🔁 4. Follow-up
- [ ] Send thank-you email + incentive.
- [ ] Create a **feedback loop** for extra thoughts.
- [ ] Use AI tools for:
  - Summarizing responses
  - Clustering recurring themes
  - Drafting follow-up scripts

---

💡 **Tip**: Keep it conversational, flexible, and curiosity-driven. The goal is to uncover the *why* behind behaviors, not just the *what*.
